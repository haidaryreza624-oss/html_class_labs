    How VS Code Builds with AI
    March 13, 2025 by Pierce Boggan
    We use AI every day to ship VS Code. It's made us so much faster that, after ten years, we just went weekly. Agents were the key that unlocked this, not just for writing code, but across every part of how the team works.
    To kick off Agent Sessions Day, I sat down with Peng Lyu, Engineering Manager on the VS Code team, to walk through how the VS Code team actually uses AI for our day-to-day work.
    In that session, we probably only covered 5% of what the team does with agents on any given day. But it's representative of how a product that's used by millions of developers is built.
